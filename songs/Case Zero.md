Cycle note:
Not a fifth part of anything. A counter-deposition that sits beside the trilogy rather than extending it, and it argues with one specific piece of text: the couplet in The Final Frontier verse 1 that disposes of the first casualty in a single line. That song is testimony compiled afterward by people who "didn't have a word for it while we were losing." This is the woman that line is about, answering it in her own voice, from after the war.

The trilogy keeps its ending intact and The Radius keeps its position as the opening of the second cycle. Nothing here resolves the war, reopens it, or supplies a sender.

The premise is the asymmetry the other four songs never look at. Every one of them is the finder's voice. She is the one it happened to, twice, in both directions, and she has no song. Her injury is not the death and not the decision. It is that she can be made certain, and that no instrument will ever tell her whose certainty that was.

Carried motifs, deliberate and load-bearing:
- "Come in" becomes "Go ahead." The cycle's word for permission to enter, inherited by a dispatcher whose professional phrase means permission to speak. Same protocol, same function, and it is hers before it is anyone's. She opens the song granting it to a stranger and closes the song granting it again, which is the whole of what she does with what happened to her. Nobody ever said it to her.
- "Did I say yes?" is returned to its owner. The cycle's most quotable line is the defender's bridge and the crowd's chant. Here it is a plain sentence said flat, twice, in a small room to five people, and nobody writes it down. It is not the hook; it is the thing the hook was made out of. Placement matters: it arrives mid-bridge, unaccompanied, and is immediately followed by the protocol she actually built from it — ask now, ask for this one thing, revocable by noon — so she is the author of the consent rule rather than its emblem.
- The choir is named as a theft and then refused. "Somebody put a choir behind my worst afternoon" is a direct reading of The Final Frontier's own final chorus with congregation underneath. A song that says that line cannot then have one. So the final chorus is fully scored and single-voiced: one woman against an arrangement that wanted a congregation, ending on "Don't make it a we." This is a different refusal from The Radius, which had no final chorus at all and got smaller. This one lets the swell arrive and denies it the crowd.
- "Where that certainty began" keeps the wanting's provenance unresolved without limiting possible sources to Nia and Mara. It asks about certainty rather than memory, from inside the event, and does not spend The Radius's terminal provenance question. The later question remains reserved for the second cycle.
- "Bit to the spectrum. Spectrum to bone" takes its fourth turn. The chain has resolved into a wave finding a home, a wire reaching bone, and a voice crossing air. Here it arrives and leaves nothing: "Something found a home in me and left no mark to find." The two lines after it — nobody can tell me it was mine, nobody can tell me it wasn't — are the balanced statement of both origin accounts, and neither is favored.
- The eight seconds belong to the receiver. She was the source before she was the casualty, and she never experienced the offset. "The eight seconds were hers. She kept them. I have never once been late to my own life." The other woman is present in the song, unnamed, only as someone holding a copy of a December morning. The song gives the listener every reason to connect that to the certainty, and no instrument that can.
- The wanting is rendered as having no perceptible moment of arrival. No voice, words, order, or traceable threshold gives her a scene to name; the pre-chorus refuses the scene the listener wants without denying that a thought arrived. "Believing is a thing you do. I never did a thing. I came out the other side already sure" is the exact distinction: she did not form a belief, she was issued a conclusion.
- The relief does not relieve. The logs are the structural surprise and they run against the shape of an exoneration. She is cleared, and the clearing is worse, because the log can only speak to the act. "The log knows what I did. The log does not know who wanted it." What gets handed back is the certainty, and she has to keep using it on strangers.
- "Case zero" is refused in the bridge, not the chorus. A table entry files a violation as a symptom. The song takes the label as its title so the thing she will not answer to is the first word on the cover.
- The record gets it wrong here and stays wrong. The song opens on the one line the history spends on her and corrects the verb rather than the attribution, because the attribution is not hers to correct. The outro moves her to the loss intake desk, where the form has a line for what came in and none for what went out. She draws the missing line by hand. That is as far as any correction goes.
- Belief follows DEC-007. Nia accepts neither unsupported origin account. Mara's high private conviction and Julian's professional inference remain outside Nia's authority and do not enter this lyric as answers. Her postwar loss-intake work demonstrates usable self-trust without factual closure, forgiveness, or archive validation.
- No sender is depicted, quoted, characterized, or given a nature. The adversary owns the war and appears here only as the absence of anything to trace.
- Reverb is characterization. The defender carries a cathedral and the visitor in The Radius has no room at all. This one lives in a hard institutional room with a short slap, a woman who has spent her life in a building with a clock in it. Her room drops away only for the bridge, where she is quoting herself, and comes back for the turn, because unlike the defender she does not get to leave.
- Static becomes the console. The Radius traded the noise floor of the spectrum for the noise floor of a life. This trades it for the noise floor of a shift: clock, keyboard, squelch, hold tone. The pre-final-chorus event has been a bar of silence, a null of two cancelling tones, and a relay click. Here it is an open channel with nobody on the other end, held too long. Nothing cancels and nothing answers.
- Namelessness is kept at the surface for consistency with the other four, though it is not a constraint on the work. She is a woman with a console. The specifics of the two calls, the unit, the county, and the person who died are held at the level of experience on purpose, so nothing in the lyric hardens detail that is still free to change.
- The Mindwars are never named. She is arguing with the histories, and refusing their brand while quoting their sentence is the sharper move.
