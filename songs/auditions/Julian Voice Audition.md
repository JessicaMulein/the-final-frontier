# Julian Voice Audition

**Technical voice-calibration material only.** This is not a song draft, a hearing, a confession scene, an in-world recording, or a Canon_Source. Every spoken line below already appears in `songs/Different Findings.md`; lines are selected and reordered only to test a generated voice.

## Run in Suno

1. Open **Custom** creation.
2. Use the neutral title **Precise Baritone Voice Audition A**. Do not put Julian's name in the Suno title or prompt.
3. Start with **no Persona, audio upload, Cover, or style reference**. This must be a newly generated voice, not a variation of Mara, Nia, Safiya, or any existing singer.
4. Keep vocals enabled. Paste the three blocks below into **Lyrics**, **Style**, and **Exclude Styles**.
5. Generate at least two unchanged batches before adjusting anything. Select for the vocalist, not the composition.

## Selection test

Keep a candidate only if all of these are true:

- The singer is unmistakably one mature male baritone and is distinct from every existing female Persona.
- The passive opening is controlled and intelligible, not robotic, pompous, or theatrically sinister.
- **“So. I helped write it”** produces only a slight increase in weight; it does not become a confession climax.
- **“Those are different findings”** remains a precise distinction, not an anthem, slogan, or courtroom victory.
- **“Enterable is not true”** lands plainly rather than as a dramatic aphorism.
- Both **“Unverified”** lines have the same low, dry weight and no flourish, uplift, whisper, or collapse.
- The same vocal identity remains stable from the passive opening through the outro.
- There are no simulated hearing sounds, legal-drama effects, or whistle artifacts.

Reject a candidate if it sounds youthful, tenor-led, operatic, villainous, absolved, openly weeping, sermon-like, lawyer-caricatured, or like a pitch-shifted version of an existing female singer. Ignore other arrangement mistakes unless they obscure or materially reshape the voice.

## Create the Persona

1. From the selected generation, use Suno's **Create Persona** action.
2. Name it **Julian — Precise Baritone**.
3. If Suno requests a description, use:

```text
Mature male baritone in a mid-low register; close, precise, level, formally plain, restrained and professionally composed, with private exhaustion beneath the surface, minimal vibrato, no theatrical remorse.
```

4. In the full `Different Findings` Custom generation, select **Julian — Precise Baritone**, paste the full song lyrics, and use the full song's own Style and Exclude Styles prompts.
5. Treat the Persona as vocal identity only. The audition's sparse arrangement is not authority for the final mix.
