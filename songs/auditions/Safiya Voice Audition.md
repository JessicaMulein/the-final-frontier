# Safiya Voice Audition

**Technical voice-calibration material only.** This is not a song draft, a scene, an in-world recording, or a Canon_Source. Every spoken line below already appears in `songs/Eleven Miles.md`; lines are selected and reordered only to test a generated voice.

## Run in Suno

1. Open **Custom** creation.
2. Use the neutral title **Dry Mezzo Voice Audition A**. Do not put Safiya's name in the Suno title or prompt.
3. Start with **no Persona, audio upload, Cover, or style reference**. This must be a newly generated voice, not a variation of Mara.
4. Keep vocals enabled. Paste the three blocks below into **Lyrics**, **Style**, and **Exclude Styles**.
5. Generate at least two unchanged batches before adjusting anything. Select for the vocalist, not the composition.

## Lyrics — paste into Lyrics

```text
[Verse 1]
I carry words for other people. I know what can cross unchanged.
I know when one mouth says the room turned
and another record gives back she felt unwell.
That is not the same answer. I stop them. We begin again.
No one stopped the sentence for me.

[Verse 2]
My public language stayed. My work stayed.
Some private words still answered. Some did not.
The loss was partial.
Partial is not small.

[Pre-Chorus]
I began the familiar inward motion.
The motion finished.
The word did not arrive.

[Chorus]
Forgetting has friction.
I know the work of grief.
You pull; it hurts; it moves.
Forgetting has friction —
and this came away so clean
there was nothing left to pull.

[Outro]
A record may hold my sentence.
It does not get to become my mouth.
Eleven miles.
That is the measure.
I am not.
```

## Selection test

Keep a candidate only if all of these are true:

- The voice is clearly distinct from Mara's and has no cathedral or ambient tail.
- The center of the voice stays adult, mid-low, close, and exact.
- **“Partial is not small”** lands as a statement, not a plea.
- **“The word did not arrive”** is controlled; the singer does not sob, whisper, or beautify the loss.
- **“I am not”** ends without a register lift, triumph, sweetness, or collapse.
- There is no whistling or whistle-like instrumental artifact anywhere in the take.
- The performance invents no culturally specific accent, ornament, or vocal tradition.

Reject a candidate if the voice is breathy, youthful, consoling, ethereal, highly vibrated, melodramatic, Mara-like, culturally inferred, or accompanied by any whistle. Ignore other arrangement mistakes at this stage unless they obscure the voice.

## Create the Persona

1. From the selected generation, use Suno's **Create Persona** action.
2. Name it **Safiya — Dry Mezzo**.
3. If Suno requests a description, use:

```text
Adult female mezzo in a mid-low register; dry, close, precise, unhurried, quietly firm, minimal vibrato, restrained rather than fragile, no culturally inferred accent or ornament.
```

4. In the full `Eleven Miles` Custom generation, select **Safiya — Dry Mezzo**, paste the full song lyrics, and use the full song's own Style and Exclude Styles prompts.
5. Treat the Persona as vocal identity only. The audition's sparse arrangement is not authority for the final mix.
