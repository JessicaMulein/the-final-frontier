Cycle note:
Third counter-account, and the first song in the cycle from the institutional side of the language. Julian is not a direct casualty and is not offered the posture of a detached observer. He helped make access saleable, then discovered the narrow boundary of what his competence could prove.

*Faraday* verse 2 puts the April offer in the mouth of an unnamed *they*: nobody on earth would be a stranger ever again, nobody would feel it, the packets would be small, think of all the good. This song places Julian among the people whose language carried that offer without making him sole author of every promise or claiming he believed every word. His narrower admission is binding: he believed the limit he wrote, failed to read page nine when he should have, traced it after it was exposed, and continued representing the position afterward.

Where it sits: beside the cycle rather than inside either arc. It does not extend the war, supply a sender, correct the indexed record, or resolve any origin account. It grants Julian neither acquittal nor redemptive effect.

Carried motifs, deliberate and load-bearing:

Julian does not steal sole authorship of the Consortium's promises, but he cannot hide his own work inside them. His belief is limited to the limit he wrote, not every word anyone drafted.
- **Page nine keeps the manuscript's chronology.** Julian does not read the specification in Chapter 44. Neither finding supplies authorship, intention, or the meaning anyone wants. The second pre-chorus presents the metadata boundary directly: unavailable before transport is not the same proposition as closure when the answer changed.
