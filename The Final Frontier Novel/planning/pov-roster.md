`DEC-012`/`DEC-015` fix four non-overlapping modes — `RECEIVE`, `INTRUDE`, `CANCEL`, and `PAIR` — that share an addressable field but not addressing, permission, bandwidth, direction of effect, or evidentiary meaning. The roster records which modes each viewpoint can testify to from inside and what that testimony cannot establish. Register discipline for the disputed `ESP` label belongs to [`voice-briefs.md`](voice-briefs.md).

| `POV-SAFIYA` | `CANCEL` only, at area scale, as a civilian with no foreign arrival | Never paired and holds no calibration; her lost layer is unavailable to `CANCEL` reversal, `PAIR`, content recording, simulation, archive, model, and reconstruction | Cancellation has no additive inverse, calibration cannot resolve to a dead person, and no corpus of the two-person layer survives |

Session ownership under `DEC-012`, `DEC-015`, and Requirements 15.2–15.3:

- Every Pairing_Session is supporting action or a deposited record inside one of the four existing POV chapters.
- `PAIR` is restricted to exactly two living participants holding current, specific, revocable mutual consent and matching pair-unique calibration; unoffered thought, imagery, memory, emotion, and background mentation stay private; pause, revocation, clipping, latency, or integrity failure stops semantic transport and requires confirmation, retry, or ordinary speech.
- Content recording starts disabled and needs explicit mutual recording consent separate from consent to pair.
- Task 5 may select living pair identities and supporting participants. It adds no sender, operative, adversary, archive, simulation, or group-mind POV, and it preserves the 56/32/33/7 loads.

A Character ID with no profile is a non-viewpoint character:

- it is **never narrated from inside**, quoted as interior experience, or given a knowledge position, moral pressure, or plot function of its own in this document;
- it is **never** the Foreign Signal or any actual, alleged, or hypothesized sender, operative, adversary, archive, simulation, model, or group mind, and no later work may promote one to a viewpoint without a documented `ArcChange` and a complete profile and brief.

The roster still contains exactly four human POVs, one Anchor, and the four Character IDs `CHAR-001` through `CHAR-004` in `POVProfile` records.
