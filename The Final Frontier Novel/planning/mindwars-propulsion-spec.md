# Mindwars Propulsion Spec — Chapters 62–112

Drafting instrument created 2026-09-12 to execute the obligations recorded in `DEC-020` clauses 1–10,
`DEC-018` clauses 8–9, `DEC-019` clauses 9–10 and 13, and `DEC-022` clause 3 across the fifty-one
undrafted Mindwars chapters.

**Status:** craft guidance. This document creates no project record, is not in the checker's record-source
allowlist, and changes no `ArcEntry` value — not one `purpose`, `hook`, `pov_id`, `timeline_id`,
`estimated_length_class`, `estimated_words`, `outlier_purpose`, `cross_cuts`, `motif_events`,
`reveal_ids`, or `record_horizon`. Under Requirement 12.12, global invariant 25 of
[`arc-outline.md`](arc-outline.md), and `DEC-021`, no checker may score, rank, threshold, or pass/fail
anything below, and no header or outline field carries a value for it. Findings live in
[`editorial-log.md`](editorial-log.md) with prose evidence.

**Why it exists.** The propulsion defect has been diagnosed four times — `DEC-016` clause 3, `DEC-018`
clauses 1–3, `DEC-020` clauses 1–9, `DEC-022` clause 3 — and executed once, in the Chapter 43 rewrite.
Fifty-one of the sixty-four undrafted chapters are Mindwars. If the remedy is not assigned to specific
chapters before Chapter 62 is drafted, it will be diagnosed a fifth time against a finished manuscript,
when it is unaffordable.

## The amendment governs: motivated, not scheduled

The 2026-09-13 amendment to `DEC-020` withdrew the fixed per-batch occurrence thresholds. Nothing below
is a quota, a rota, or a checklist slot. Every assignment is derived from that chapter's existing
`purpose` and `hook` and is justified by them. Where an assignment stops serving the scene, the scene
wins and the assignment is dropped — but the cluster-level obligation it was carrying must then land
somewhere else in the same cluster, deliberately.

Spacing must stay irregular. If a reader can predict which chapter is the short one or which ending
interrupts, the remedy has become the disease.

## The two diagnosed troughs

These are the two places the plan as written will fail, and they are visible in the ArcEntries without
reading any prose.

**Trough one — 62 to 69.** Eight consecutive chapters whose events are: reports read, a flag refused, a
file opened, a demonstration case refused, arrivals compared, a category funded, an experiment returning
nothing, doctrine issued. Every one is an institutional process. No body, no location under pressure, no
physical consequence anywhere in the onset of a war. Julian holds 64, 67 and 69 — three document chapters
inside eight — and 64 through 69 is arguably six consecutive institutional-process chapters, which is the
condition `DEC-020` clause 8 exists to forbid. This stretch is worse than the Chapters 14/15/22/26 trough
that `DEC-020` was written to fix, and it is the first thing a reader meets after the Private Defense
movement ends.

**Trough two — 94 to 101.** The moral climax of the novel is eight chapters of deliberation: enrollment
pressure, a model result, an annex discovered, an attempt to aim, a refusal of unanimity, an
authorization written, a name entered in a field, a cost stated aloud. The sequence is correct and it is
the best-designed decision in the book. It is also, structurally, eight chapters of people arguing about
whether to do a thing — which is precisely the engine `DEC-020` clause 1 says may not dominate, because
a deliberation deadline has no physical consequence attached to its expiry.

Both troughs get the same medicine: **the thing being deliberated about must be actively hurting people
on the page while the deliberation proceeds.**

## The physical-jeopardy engine already exists

`DEC-020` clause 5 requires bodies across 62–112 and clause 3 of the retired alternatives forbids a genre
transplant. Both are satisfiable without inventing anything, because the mechanism itself is a
jeopardy engine and the book has not yet used it.

`INTRUDE` alters certainty, urgency, wanting and preference. People act on certainty. Chapter 61 already
ends with a rail operator putting three sections at stop, a plant operator closing an intake and
dropping pressure at a clinic, and a shift controller halting a picking line — three people made certain
of something false, with real consequences downstream. Those arrived as **paper**: forwarded summaries,
written by managers, about people who had gone home.

In Mindwars they happen in front of a viewpoint. That is the whole conversion:

- an arrival makes someone certain, and the person acts with their body, at speed, on a road, a
  platform, a ladder, a gantry, a ward, a plant floor, or a kerb;
- the consequence lands in tissue and time rather than in a file;
- nobody can be negotiated with afterwards, because `INTRUDE` carries no proposition and there is
  nothing to answer;
- and a viewpoint character is close enough to be hurt, to fail to prevent it, or to be the one who
  acted.

Four further legitimate sources, all inside canon: crowds and queues at shielded facilities, where
scarcity becomes crush and temper; the copper rooms themselves, which are hot, sealed, airless and
openable only from inside; operators holding an emitter or a contact band for hours under load; and
travel between sites during an active event, which is movement under pressure through a country where
strangers are becoming certain of things.

Prohibited throughout, restating `DEC-020` clause 5 and the standing boundaries: no spectacle, no
pursuit written for its own sake, no combatant with a face, no Foreign_Signal agent, no sender POV, and
no scene in which physical danger supplies provenance.

## Per-chapter assignment

Legend. **Clock:** `C` consequence clock — an irreversible external event that occurs whether or not the
narrator decides anything; `D` deliberation clock, permitted but never dominant. **End:** `INT`
interrupted act, `OBJ` arriving object or document, `ANS` answer given aloud, `IMG` physical image with
no gloss, `OTH` consequence landing on someone other than the narrator, `REF` refusal, `DEL`
deliberation deadline. **✂** interrupted cut. **†** body, jeopardy, or physical consequence on the page.
**◆** Dalby acts rather than argues. **♥** non-professional warmth, food, rest, comfort, or humour that
is not a professional riposte. **$** a personal, non-abstract cost to that chapter's narrator.

## Budget guard

Current measured state: 64 delivered chapters, 71,931 Prose_Words. Class means are 1,145.7 `normal`,
512.4 `microchapter`, 1,737.3 `long-outlier`. Remaining planned: 54 `normal`, 6 `microchapter`, 4
`long-outlier`. Projected completion is about **143,800** against the approved 130,000–150,000
Final_Targets, leaving roughly **6,200 words of headroom**.

That headroom is the constraint on everything above, because every obligation here pushes length upward.
Three rules follow:

1. **`DEC-019` clause 9 is a substitution, not an addition.** Writing an event as continuous scene means
   the narrated summary of that event is deleted. A chapter that gains a dramatized negotiation and
   keeps its summary of the negotiation has done the wrong half of the work.
2. **Microchapters stay micro.** 72, 103, 105 and 107 carry compression as their `outlier_purpose`.
   Chapter 105 in particular is assigned careful handling above, and careful is not the same as longer.
3. **Re-measure at 77, 93 and 108.** If the projection passes 147,000 at any checkpoint, the repair is
   allocation at the cluster that overran, per `DEC-021` clause 4 — never uniform trimming, and never
   cutting the bodies back out.

The planned length-class distribution is 108 `normal`, 11 `microchapter`, 9 `long-outlier`, which sits
**exactly at the 108-entry `normal` floor and exactly at the 20-outlier ceiling**. There is no planning
headroom for a new outlier: promoting any chapter above needs an `ArcChange`. Delivery is currently
drifting the safe way, since Chapters 17 and 56 were planned `long-outlier` and delivered `normal`.
