I drafted the repaired rule in five parts.

First, access to a room, console, programme, or service authorizes property use only. It is not pairing consent.

Second, pairing consent names two living people, one current session, and one specific scope. It expires when the session ends and cannot be cached into a later question.

Third, every deliberate send requires current mutual authorization for that act or for a bounded class of acts already stated and still accepted. The interface may not solicit or accept a send request until the live-consent gate has confirmed both current states.

Fourth, acknowledgment proves receipt only. It supplies no agreement, renewal, or permission for a next contribution.

Fifth, content recording remains off unless both participants separately enable it for that session. Consent to pair and consent to send do not imply consent to preserve words.
