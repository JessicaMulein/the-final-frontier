# The Final Frontier — v0.9.0

The first public release: all 128 chapters delivered, a full AI-narrated
audiobook, and the songs its world grew from. Free to read, free to listen.

This is v0.9.0, not 1.0 — a complete draft, deliberately. Under the project's own
rules nothing is `final` until it passes both an objective checker and a human
editorial review, and that editing pass has not happened yet. The version number
says so on purpose.

> Somewhere behind the eyes there's a country with no army,
> and one small question standing in the door.

A story about neural privacy and consent — who gets to reach a thought, and who
gets to say no. About 150,000 words in 128 chapters.

## What's in this release

**Read**
- `the-final-frontier.epub` — EPUB 3 with EPUB Accessibility 1.1 metadata
- `the-final-frontier.pdf` — print-style PDF
- `the-final-frontier.md` — the whole book as a single Markdown file

**Listen**
- `the-final-frontier.m4b` — the chaptered audiobook, ~13.4 hours, 128 chapters
  with spoken chapter announcements for navigation
- `the-final-frontier-mp3.zip` — all 128 chapters as individual MP3s
- `the-final-frontier.m4b.json` — sidecar describing what the M4B contains

**Verify**
- `the-final-frontier-manifests.zip` — the per-chapter production manifests
  (identity hashes, quality metrics, patch records), so the audiobook can be
  checked without re-rendering it
- `SHA256SUMS` — checksums for every asset above

## Why the audiobook exists

The narration is licensed under the same terms as the prose, on purpose. The
book's argument is that thought and consent belong to the person, not to whoever
can reach them; that argument should reach anyone, including readers who cannot
use print. The audiobook is the whole book, not a summary.

The narrator is a cloned voice. Every chapter was transcribed back and checked
against the manuscript word by word so that nothing the author wrote goes
unspoken. A small number of chapters had a dropped phrase repaired with a
separately verified re-recording in the same voice; those repairs are recorded
in the manifests.

## Honest status

This is a **complete draft with a published defect list**, not a finished book.
Under the project's own rules a chapter is `final` only after an objective
checker and a human editorial review both pass; all 128 chapters are delivered
and sit at `revised`. What's missing is the editing, itemised in
[KNOWN-ISSUES.md](https://github.com/JessicaMulein/the-final-frontier/blob/main/KNOWN-ISSUES.md).
The prose was drafted by large language models against a written specification
and revised under the author's direction; the full account is in
[METHOD.md](https://github.com/JessicaMulein/the-final-frontier/blob/main/METHOD.md).

If you find something broken that isn't on the list, please open an issue.
