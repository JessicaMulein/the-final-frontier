She wondered how she had been able to sit daily in front of that stout and earnest index without poking it in the ribs and making a fool of it. I think if the poor thing had possessed as many legs as my editor tells me somebody called the Hydra (with whom my readers are perhaps more familiar than I am) had heads, She ought to have arranged it in some way to get them quietly home as soon as she found out what kind of cousin it was that had come to see them.
